## What Changed in v2

- Added 41 recent records, mostly from 2024-2026.
- Added recent high-priority GO/graphene records including pyridinic-graphene membranes, crumpled GO membranes, graphene quantum dot membranes, GO/melamine/ionic-liquid membranes, and GO laminate thickness studies.
- Added 2024-2026 MOF entries including orientation-controlled MOF membranes, 0D-on-2D MOF membranes, MOF nanosheet rigidity control, vapor-phase crystalline transformation, and 2D ZIF-8 nanosheet MMMs.
- Added recent COF entries including molecular weaving, solvent-switch-driven COF nanosheets, iCOF@PEI ultrathin composite membranes, and newer COF membrane reviews.
- Added MXene/rGO, DES/nanocellulose-tuned MXene, MXene-PDMS, ZIF-8@MXene, and MXene CO2-separation review records.
- Added graphdiyne, g-C3N4, MoS2, and zeolite/MOF composite records to widen the non-GO/MOF/COF 2D-material coverage.

## Notes

- `priority=high` means the paper is likely useful for early manual curation or benchmark extraction.
- This file deliberately separates candidate bibliography from `data/curated/`; values such as permeance, permeability, selectivity, pressure, humidity, and stability still need paper-level extraction and validation.
- The next pass should screen abstracts and full texts for real gas-separation relevance, then convert high-priority papers into manually curated benchmark records.
